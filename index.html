CYNORAPHONK Autopost

Personal tool by Cynorra

Turns the creator's own original phonk tracks into short vertical clips and publishes them to the creator's own TikTok account on a daily schedule.

Step 1 – Connect your TikTok account

The creator signs in once with TikTok Login Kit and grants permission to publish videos to their own account.

Scopes requested: user.info.basic, video.publish

How it works

  1. Connect the TikTok account (this page).
  2. Once a day a clip is generated from one of the creator's tracks.
  3. The clip is uploaded and published to the connected account with the TikTok Content Posting API.
